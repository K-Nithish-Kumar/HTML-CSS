Chicken Biryani Preparation

To make chicken biryani

Marinate and Cook Chicken

  • Clean and wash the chicken pieces well.
  • Mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, turmeric, salt, and biryani masala with the chicken.
  • Rest the chicken for at least 30 to 60 minutes.
  • Heat oil or ghee in a heavy pot.
  • Fry sliced onions until golden brown (birista) and remove half for later.
  • Add whole spices (cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, bay leaves) to the pot.
  • Add the marinated chicken and cook until tender and the oil separates.

Boil Rice

  • Wash and soak basmati rice for 30 minutes.
  • Boil water in a separate large pot with whole spices and salt.
  • Add the soaked rice and cook until it is 70 to 80 percent done (partially cooked).
  • Drain the water completely

Layer and Dum Cook

  • Grease a heavy-bottom pot with ghee.
  • Add a layer of cooked chicken and gravy at the bottom.
  • Add a layer of partially cooked rice over the chicken.
  • Top with fried onions, chopped mint, coriander leaves, and saffron milk.
  • Seal the pot tightly with a tight lid or foil.
  • Cook on very low heat (dum) for 15 to 20 minutes.
  • Rest for 10 minutes before opening and serving hot.
