K.G.F: Chapter 2

Basic Information

Main Cast

1. Yash — Rocky / Raja Krishnappa Bairya

Yash Image

The central character of the movie. Rocky has now become the ruler of KGF after defeating Garuda in Chapter 1. His ambition is no longer simply to become rich—he wants to become the most powerful person in the world. Yash's performance is one of the biggest strengths of the film. His body language, dialogue delivery, screen presence and action sequences are designed around the larger-than-life Rocky character. Even critics who disliked aspects of the film generally acknowledged Yash's commanding performance.

2. Sanjay Dutt — Adheera

Sanjay Dutt Image

The primary antagonist. Adheera is Garuda's uncle and one of the most dangerous people connected to KGF. He returns to claim control over the gold fields. Sanjay Dutt brings a very different energy compared with Yash. His character is brutal, intimidating and physically imposing. His introduction and confrontation scenes with Rocky are among the movie's major highlights.

3. Raveena Tandon — Ramika Sen

Raveena Tandon Image

She plays Ramika Sen, the Prime Minister of India. Unlike Rocky and Adheera, she represents the power of the government. She considers Rocky a major threat and wants to destroy his empire.

4. Srinidhi Shetty — Reena Desai

Srinidhi Shetty Image

Reena is Rocky's love interest. Her character has considerably less importance compared with Rocky, Adheera and Ramika Sen. Several reviews specifically criticized the film for not developing Rocky and Reena's relationship enough.

Story — Without Major Spoilers

After the events of Chapter 1, Rocky has taken control of KGF. But becoming the king isn't the end of his journey. Now he has three major problems: Adheera wants KGF. Ramika Sen wants Rocky eliminated. And Rocky himself wants to expand his power far beyond KGF. So the story becomes a battle between: Rocky vs Adheera vs the Government At the same time, the movie continues exploring Rocky's relationship with his mother, his people, Reena and his obsession with power. The movie essentially asks: How far can Rocky go to become the most powerful man in the world?
